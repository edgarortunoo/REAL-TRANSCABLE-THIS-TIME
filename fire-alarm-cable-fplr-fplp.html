Fire Alarm Cable

Fire alarm cable built in Bonham, Texas for life-safety systems. Available in FPLP plenum and FPLR riser ratings, shielded and unshielded configurations, stocked across the standard conductor counts.

Plenum & riser

FPLP for plenum spaces, FPLR for riser runs. Both stocked in shielded and unshielded constructions.

Solid copper

Annealed bare copper conductors for reliable, low-resistance signaling.

Cut to spec

Sold per foot or on reels, cut and labeled to your pull list.

Applications

  • Fire alarm and life safety systems
  • Notification appliance circuits
  • Smoke and heat detection loops
  • Pull stations and NAC circuits
